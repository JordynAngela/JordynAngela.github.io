About Biostats WILD

Biostats WILD is my 2025 BI393 undergraduate biostatistics portfolio.

The portfolio explores biological datasets, statistical methods, research questions, data visualization, and the creative side of working with scientific data.

Rather than treating statistics as simply a collection of formulas, this portfolio documents my process of learning how statistical thinking can be used to investigate biological and psychological questions.
